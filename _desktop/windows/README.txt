COLE'S GAMES for Windows
========================
Blackjack DX, Lines DX, Lucky Flip and Sky Glider, plus a player for all the game music. Works offline.
For Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit).

1. Right-click the zip and choose "Extract All", then open the "Cole's Games" folder.
2. Double-click "Cole's Games.exe".
3. The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't signed with a paid
   certificate. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
Tip: right-click "Cole's Games.exe" and choose "Pin to Start" or "Pin to taskbar".

Shortcuts: Ctrl+0 Home, Ctrl+1 Blackjack DX, Ctrl+2 Lines DX, Ctrl+3 Lucky Flip, Ctrl+4 Music,
Ctrl+R restart the game, F11 full screen. The menu button at the right of the toolbar has the rest.

Your progress saves on this PC. The app never connects to the internet.
