COLE'S GAMES for Linux
======================
Blackjack DX, Lines DX and Lucky Flip, plus a player for all the game music. Works offline.
For 64-bit Linux (Ubuntu, Fedora, Mint, Debian, Arch and others).

1. Extract the download, then open a terminal in the "Cole's Games" folder.
2. Run:      ./start.sh
3. To add it to your app menu:      ./install.sh
   (Move the folder where you want to keep it first, like your home folder.)

Shortcuts: Ctrl+0 Home, Ctrl+1 Blackjack DX, Ctrl+2 Lines DX, Ctrl+3 Lucky Flip, Ctrl+4 Music,
Ctrl+R restart the game, F11 full screen. The menu button at the right of the toolbar has the rest.

Your progress saves on this computer. The app never connects to the internet.
