How to Play

Every tile hides a 1, 2, 3 or a bomb . Click a tile to flip it. The first number you flip becomes your coins, and every number after that multiplies them. 1s don't hurt, 2s double you, 3s triple you.

The boxes on the edge are your clues: the top number is what that row or column adds up to, and the bottom is how many bombs are hiding in it. A row with 0 bombs is free money. A row that adds up to 5 with 0 bombs is all 1s, so don't bother.

Find every 2 and 3 to clear the level and bank your coins. Flip a bomb and you lose this round's coins, and you might drop a level. Getting nervous? Cash Out keeps what you've got. Higher levels hide more bombs but pay way more. There are 8 levels.

Special tiles show up now and then: a gold 5 multiplies your coins by 5, a dud (½) cuts them in half, a ? tile is a gamble that's either a 4 or a bomb (you don't need to flip it), and a chain tile flips the safe tiles next to it for free. Clues count gold as 5, a winning ? as 4 and a dud as 0.

Flip three 2s or 3s in a row and you're on a Hot Streak: the next tile counts double. On the way up you'll run into boss boards (a bigger board, clues lost in fog, bombs that move), and clearing one pays double.

Spend your bank in the Shop on new card backs, bombs, table felt, flip sounds and one-use helpers. Play the Daily board (same for everyone, one try a day), knock out your 3 daily Goals for bonus coins, and fill up your trophy shelf.

Hard Mode: more bombs, no Notes, no helpers, and a bomb sends you all the way back to level 1. Every payout counts double, it keeps its own best, and it has its own song.

Turn on Notes to mark what you think a tile is without flipping it. Keyboard works too: arrows to move, Space to flip, N for notes, and B / 1 / 2 / 3 to mark.Hold down on a tile to quickly mark it as a bomb.
