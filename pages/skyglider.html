How to Play

Fly Glider through the cloud maze and grab every sparkle to clear the level. The storm clouds are out to get you, so don't let them touch you!

Grab a sun and the storms get scared and puff up white. Fly into them for big points: 200, 400, 800, then 1600 for the whole set. Keep grabbing sparkles without a break to build your combo, all the way up to x5.

Power-ups pop up in the maze: Jet makes you faster, Magnet pulls in sparkles, Shield blocks one storm, Snow freezes the storms, 2x doubles your points, and if you're lucky a heart gives you an extra life. Grab the same one again while it's running and it stacks, up to 3: two Jets is way faster, two 2x is 4x points. When Glider goes down you can steer him a little on the way, so aim for a sparkle.

Every level gets a little faster, and so does every second you spend on one. Earn stars every flight to buy new paint jobs and trails in the Hangar, and check your Flight Goals for bonus stars. The Daily Flight is the same for everyone and you only get one life.

Keyboard: arrow keys or WASD to fly, Space to start, P to pause, M to mute. On a phone, swipe anywhere, tap the maze where you want to go, or hold the arrow pad and slide your thumb around it to steer.

Credits: Glider made by cherrie/sqeakz on Instagram. Concept and game made by Cole.
