How to Play

Click the chips to bet, then hit Deal. Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Face cards are worth 10 and Aces are 1 or 11.

Hit takes another card. Stand keeps what you have. Double doubles your bet for exactly one more card. Split turns a pair into two hands. The dealer draws until 17.

A win pays your bet back double. A Blackjack (an Ace and a 10 card) pays 3 to 2. The Lucky Card changes every hand: get dealt it in your first two cards for a free bonus, or get two for 10x!

Win hands in a row to build Heat: your winnings get multiplied, all the way up to 3x. Every hand earns XP, and leveling up gives you chips and unlocks bigger ones. Come back every day for free chips.

Keyboard: 1-5 for chips, Space to deal, H / S / D / P to hit, stand, double and split. The chips are just for fun and can't be bought or cashed out.
